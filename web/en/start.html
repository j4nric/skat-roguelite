Real Skat by the official rules, with the German deck. Then house rules, marked cards and the landlord's schnapps change everything you know about Skat. Crawl from pub to pub through the city and beat the bosses at the regulars' table.

Real Skat.

Pick up the Skat or play Hand, discard, declare: the game follows the official Skat rules, with Kontra and Re, Schneider and Schwarz, Grand and Null. Acorns, Leaves, Hearts and Bells; Unter, Ober and Ace.

New to the table? The landlord teaches you the game in a short introduction. A game takes less than two minutes.

Break the rules.

The score is counted like in Skat: base value times levels. And then come the multipliers. More than 80 house rules and locals bend the sum until a Bells Hand is worth 342.

Every score runs line by line down the slip. One tap and it's done.

Marked cards, the landlord's schnapps.

At the bar you buy house rules, mark cards and order schnapps: a Wee Dram shows you the Skat, after a Double Korn the cards are dealt again and the next game counts double. Some cards turn undead, some cursed.

At the end of every district a boss waits with a rule of his own. There are seven, from the Twins to the Old Man.

Different every night.

  • Your own route through the city's pubs, on foot, by train or by bike.
  • Seven characters with their own style, from the Declarer to Aunt Ramsch.
  • A daily run every day, a challenge with a special rule every week.
  • Beat the Old Man and carry on into the long night.
