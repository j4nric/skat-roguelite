Short description

Noch 'ne Runde Skat is Skat as a roguelike: real Skat by the official rules, plus more than 80 house rules and locals, marked cards and the landlord's schnapps that blow up the score. Play Skat. Break the rules.

Description

A night out in the city, one pub after the other, and at every table they play Skat, Germany's favourite card game. Noch 'ne Runde Skat takes the game seriously: pick up the Skat or play Hand, discard, declare, ten tricks, scored by the official Skat rules with the German deck. Never played Skat? The landlord teaches you in a short introduction.

Then come the house rules. Every pub wants to see a target score, and only bigger games get you there: house rules and locals bend base value, levels and multipliers, at the bar you mark cards and order schnapps. Some cards turn undead, some cursed. Every score runs line by line down the slip until the stamp comes down.

At the end of every district a boss waits with a rule of his own; there are seven. Seven characters each play differently, from the Declarer to Aunt Ramsch. Add a daily run that everyone plays with the same cards, a challenge of the week and, for whoever beats the Old Man, the long night. A game takes less than two minutes.

Features

  • Real Skat by the official rules: suit games, Grand, Null, Kontra and Re, Schneider and Schwarz
  • More than 80 house rules and locals, marked and undead cards, schnapps
  • Seven bosses with their own rules, seven playable characters
  • Daily run, challenge of the week, the long night
  • Pixel cards in the German suits, drawn for the game
  • English and German, no ads, no tracking, plays offline
